Damien Hirst · 1991

A shark that cannot die, and cannot live

Hirst hung a 4.3-metre tiger shark in a tank of formaldehyde, eyes open, as if it might still move. It did not stay that way: the fluid clouded, in 1993 its skin was stretched over a fibreglass mould, and in 2006 Hirst replaced it with a new shark in the same tank. If the idea is the work, the shark can be swapped. If the shark is the work, the 1991 piece is gone.

How the film makes it move

Behind the glass, six pictures are cut into thin strips and woven together. Each film is black with clear slits that let only one picture's strips through. Slide the film and the next picture shows — the dead shark moves only while you move it.
