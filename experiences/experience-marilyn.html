Marilyn Diptych

Andy Warhol · 1962 · light, repeated

The field

62
74

Press and drag across the floor to send rings out. Tap her and she answers; drag her to turn her round. Drag the dark sky to walk round the room.

Colour, heard

Red — low, warm
Yellow — open fifth
Green — soft bell
Blue — high chime
Violet — glassy

Warhol printed one face over and over, each time in another colour. Here the colour is what you hear.

The crystal

60
50

Your pointer is a light: move it over her and it shines into the glass, splitting into colour inside her. Drag her to turn her, tap to strike the glass. Drag the dark to walk round her.

Glass, heard

Red — singing bowl
Orange — wine glass
Yellow — a tapped glass
Green — glass chime
Blue — crystal bell
Violet — glass harmonica

Every facet splits the light into its colours, and every colour rings its own glass.

The fog

60
50

Drag through her and the mist follows your hand, slowly, bending her out of shape like a dream. Tap and the next colour of her comes forward.

Her colours

Pink, then blue, then a dark figure rimmed in light, then the prism: each colour of her gathers out of the smoke as Marilyn, holds, and goes back into it while the next arrives.
