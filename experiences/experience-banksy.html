Paint with your hand

You appear in front of a real wall — only you, cut out of the camera picture (or paint on the plain camera view). It watches your hands — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

  1. ✋Open your hand — the cans and stencils come out, and stay
  2. ☝️Point your index finger and it sprays where it points
  3. ✋☝️To choose a colour, stencil or wall, hold your other hand wide open and point — nothing is painted while you choose
  4. 👍Thumb up for a bigger, heavier spray — thumb down for smaller
  5. ✊For a stencil, bring your fist down — hold a still fist to put the cans away
